Consult a physician before beginning judo or any physical activity program, particularly if you or your child has a medical condition, a prior injury, or has been inactive.

5. Assumption of risk

Judo is a physical contact sport with inherent risks. Participants are thrown to the mat, grip and grapple with partners, and practice pins and — for adults — chokes and joint locks. Even with qualified instruction, safety rules, and proper facilities, participation carries risk of injury including but not limited to bruises, sprains, strains, dislocations, fractures, concussion, and, in rare cases, serious or catastrophic injury.

Every student begins with ukemi — learning to fall safely — and our instructors are experienced, certified black belts who supervise all training. Risk can be reduced, but it cannot be eliminated. Participants, and the parents or guardians of minor participants, accept these risks knowingly and voluntarily.

Registration, waivers, liability releases, and medical or emergency-contact forms are administered by the City of Petaluma Parks & Recreation Department, and by our affiliated organizations for membership and tournament purposes. Those documents — not this website — govern participation. Nothing on this page replaces or modifies any waiver or release you sign.
