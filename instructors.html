Our instructors

Volunteer black belts with decades of combined experience. Several of them grew up in this dojo.

Rokudan, 6th dan · Head instructor

Sensei Henry Kaku

Sensei Kaku holds his black belt from the Kodokan and has over 50 years in judo, more than 25 of them teaching. He competed nationally, including High School Nationals and AAU Nationals, and was on the San Jose State University varsity judo team.

He's a life member of the United States Judo Federation and chairs the Board of Examiners of the Central Coast Judo Association (CENCO), which oversees rank standards across its ten member clubs. A former math teacher and high school counselor, he has led DeLeon Judo Club since 2007.

Godan, 5th dan · Assistant head instructor

Sensei Davin Tillman

Davin began training at DeLeon Judo Club in 1992, at age 5. He's President of CENCO's Board of Managers, a certified regional referee, and sits on the club's board.

Visiting senpai

Black belts, former students and friends of the club, who drop in to train and help teach. Judo is a small world, and the dojo is better for every one of them.

Train with them.

Every instructor here volunteers their time, and every class has several of them on the mat, so beginners get individual attention. Come meet them any Tuesday or Thursday at 5:30 pm.
