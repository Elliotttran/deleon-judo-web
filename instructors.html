Our Instructors

Volunteer black belts with decades of combined experience. Several of them grew up in this dojo.

Sensei Henry Kaku Rokudan · 6th Dan

Head Instructor

Sensei Kaku holds his black belt from Kodokan Judo and has over 50 years of experience in judo and more than 25 years teaching. He competed at the national level, including High School Nationals and AAU Nationals, and was a member of the San Jose State University varsity judo team. He is a life member of the United States Judo Federation (USJF) and chairs the Board of Examiners of the Central Coast Judo Association (CENCO), our USJF yudanshakai — the body that oversees rank standards across its ten member clubs. He holds teaching credentials, having worked as a math teacher and high school counselor, and has led DeLeon Judo Club since 2007.

Sensei Davin Tillman Yondan · 4th Dan

Assistant Head Instructor

Davin began training with DeLeon Judo Club in 1992 at the age of 5. He earned Shodan in 2008, Nidan in 2011, Sandan in 2017, and Yondan in 2022. He serves as President of the Board of Managers of the Central Coast Judo Association (CENCO), our USJF yudanshakai, is a certified regional referee, and sits on the club's board.

Visiting senpai

We're also fortunate to have other judo black belts — former students and friends of the club — who drop in to train and help teach from time to time. Judo is a small world, and the dojo is better for every one of them.
