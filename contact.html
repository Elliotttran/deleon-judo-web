Contact Us

The best way to reach us is to come by a class — Tuesdays and Thursdays at 5:30 pm. No appointment needed, and no need to call ahead.

Registering

We don't take sign-ups or payments on this site. Everything goes through the Petaluma Parks & Recreation catalog — search for "Judo."

Visiting

Come watch any Tuesday or Thursday 5:30 pm class. Herzog Hall is on the Sonoma-Marin Fairgrounds off Fairgrounds Drive; parking is on site.

Private sessions

Tuesday and Thursday 7:00–8:30 pm sessions are by appointment. Ask Sensei Kaku or Sensei Tillman at the end of a regular class to arrange one.
