Contact us

The best way to reach us is to come by a class, Tuesdays and Thursdays at 5:30 pm. No appointment needed, and no need to call ahead.

Registering

We don't take sign-ups or payments on this site. Everything goes through the Petaluma Parks & Recreation catalog. Search for "Judo."

Open the catalog

Visiting

Come watch any Tuesday or Thursday 5:30 pm class. Herzog Hall is on the Sonoma-Marin Fairgrounds off Fairgrounds Drive, with parking on site.

Directions

Private sessions

Monday sessions, 5:30 – 7:00 pm, are by appointment. Ask Sensei Kaku or Sensei Tillman at the end of a regular class to arrange one.
