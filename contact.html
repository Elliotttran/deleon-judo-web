Contact Us

The best way to reach us is to come by a class. Otherwise, call or email one of the instructors.

Registering

We don't take sign-ups or payments on this site. Everything goes through the Petaluma Parks & Recreation catalog — search for "Judo."

Visiting

Come watch any Tuesday or Thursday 5:30 pm class. The Kenilworth Teen Center is on the Sonoma-Marin Fairgrounds off Fairgrounds Drive; parking is on site.

Private sessions

Tuesday and Thursday 7:00–8:30 pm sessions are by appointment. Call Sensei Kaku or Sensei Tillman to arrange one.
