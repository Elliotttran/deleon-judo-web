Judo for kids

Ages 6 and up. A sport that builds confidence, body awareness and respect, and it's a lot of fun.

Why judo for your child?

An Olympic sport with no punching or kicking. The goal is to throw, pin and control with balance and leverage, which suits kids of every size and temperament.

Fall safely

The first and most useful skill in judo, on and off the mat.

Show respect

For partners, instructors and the traditions of the dojo: bowing, etiquette, Japanese terms.

Keep at it

Belts are earned through steady effort, not bought.

Join a team

In this club the older students look out for the younger ones.

We know what it means to grow up in this dojo. Our assistant head instructor, Sensei Davin Tillman, started at DeLeon in 1992, at age 5.

Safety is built into how judo is taught.

Judo is a contact sport. Every class is supervised by certified black-belt instructors with decades of experience teaching kids.

Parents' questions

Falling comes first

Students learn to fall before they learn to throw.

Cooperative practice

Techniques are practised slowly and together with a partner.

Randori when ready

Free practice is introduced only when a student is ready for it.

Age-appropriate

Chokes and arm-locks are not taught to young children.

Your child's first class

Parents are welcome to watch. Come by any Tuesday or Thursday to see a class before you sign up.

Arrive a few minutes early

Sweatpants and a t-shirt with no zippers or metal, a water bottle, and flip-flops for the walk to the mat. Nails trimmed, no jewelry.

Bow in and warm up

Class starts with a bow and a group warm-up. New students then work with an instructor on ukemi, how to fall without getting hurt.

Learn a first technique

Most kids learn their first throw and pin on day one, practised slowly and cooperatively with a partner.

Decide if it's a fit

If your child enjoys it, register through Petaluma Parks & Rec. We'll help you find a gi.

Bring your kid to watch a class.

Parents are always welcome on the sidelines. Come by any Tuesday or Thursday at 5:30 pm and see whether it's a fit before you sign up.
