About DeLeon Judo Club

A volunteer-run, non-profit dojo serving Petaluma since January 1970.

Our history

Generations of Petaluma families have learned judo here. Many of today's instructors first stepped on the mat as children.

  1. 1970

    Jim Santiago DeLeon founds the club in January.

  2. 1992

    Davin Tillman starts training here at age 5. Today he's our assistant head instructor.

  3. 2007

    Sensei Henry Kaku, a Kodokan black belt with over 50 years in judo, takes the lead.

  4. Today

    A non-profit run with the City of Petaluma Parks & Recreation Department, which handles registration.

Meet our instructors

Our philosophy

We teach the physical skills of judo along with its philosophy and traditions. Judo, "the gentle way," was developed by Dr. Jigoro Kano and uses leverage, balance, movement and the opponent's own strength.

Maximum efficiency 精力善用

Use the least physical strength necessary, through proper technique and timing.

Mutual welfare and benefit 自他共栄

The discipline of judo reaches beyond the dojo into daily life, and we look out for each other.

We introduce judo as a lifetime pursuit, a skilled art that's never finished. We keep traditional etiquette and the Japanese names of techniques to honour the art's heritage. A dojo is a place of stability and community, where people watch out for you, on the mat and off it.

Our affiliations

We're not an independent school with our own system. We teach Kodokan judo and belong to the national organizations that govern it in the United States.

The rank you earn here is certified and portable. It's recognised at any judo club in the country and by clubs abroad. Your instructors are ranked through the same system, and students who want to compete can enter sanctioned tournaments up to the national championships.

How promotion works

Membership in a national organization is separate from your Parks & Rec class registration. Instructors will tell you when and how to sign up. It's generally needed before a promotion or a tournament, not before your first class.

Our yudanshakai

Every USJF club belongs to a regional black belt association. Ours is the Central Coast Judo Association, and DeLeon instructors help lead it.

Those two seats matter for our students. The Board of Examiners sets and oversees rank standards across CENCO's member clubs, and the Board of Managers runs the association's tournaments, clinics and business. Our students learn the standard they'll be examined against from the people who help set it.

A 501(c)(3) non-profit club

Volunteer instructors, fees that go back into the program, and family discounts for households with more than one student.
