About DeLeon Judo Club

A volunteer-run, non-profit dojo serving Petaluma since January 1970.

Our history

DeLeon Judo Club was founded in January 1970 by Jim Santiago DeLeon. For more than five decades the club has taught judo to generations of Petaluma families — many of today's instructors first stepped on the mat here as children.

Since 2007 the club has been led by Sensei Henry Kaku, a Kodokan black belt with over 50 years in judo. The club operates as a non-profit in partnership with the City of Petaluma Parks & Recreation Department, which handles all registration.

Our philosophy

Our goal is to impart to our students not only the physical skills of judo, but its philosophy and traditions. Judo — "The Gentle Way" — was developed by Dr. Jigoro Kano and makes use of leverage, balance, movement, and the opponent's own strength.

Its two core principles guide everything we do:

  • Maximum Efficiency — use the least physical strength necessary, through proper technique and timing.
  • Mutual Welfare and Benefit — the discipline of judo extends beyond the dojo into daily life, and we look out for each other.

We introduce students to judo as a lifetime pursuit, a skilled art requiring infinite perfecting. We keep traditional etiquette and the Japanese names of techniques to honor the art's heritage. A judo dojo is a place of stability and community, where people watch out for you, in and out of the dojo.

Our instructors

Certified, experienced, and volunteer. Several grew up in this dojo.

Sensei Henry Kaku

Head Instructor · Godan (5th Dan)

Sensei Kaku holds his black belt from Kodokan Judo and has over 50 years of experience in judo and more than 25 years teaching. He competed at the national level, including High School Nationals and AAU Nationals, and was a member of the San Jose State University varsity judo team. He is a life member of the United States Judo Federation (USJF) and holds teaching credentials, having worked as a math teacher and high school counselor. He has led DeLeon Judo Club since 2007.

Sensei Davin Tillman

Assistant Head Instructor · Yondan (4th Dan)

Davin began training with DeLeon Judo Club in 1992 at the age of 5. He earned Shodan in 2008, Nidan in 2011, Sandan in 2017, and Yondan in 2022. He serves as President of the Central California Judo Association (CCJA), is a certified regional referee, and sits on the club's board.

Sensei Ryan Sorensen

Assistant Instructor · Nidan (2nd Dan)

Ryan started judo with DeLeon Judo Club in 1999 at age 7. He earned Shodan in November 2014 and Nidan in May 2023.

Sensei Alma Valverde

Assistant Instructor · Shodan (1st Dan)

A club member since 2009. After a year away earning her Master's Degree in Education from Harvard, she returned to DeLeon as an assistant instructor. She earned Shodan in November 2017 and serves on the club's board.

Sensei Ion "Mickey" Mihalca

Assistant Instructor · Shodan (1st Dan)

Mickey trained in Shotokan Karate in Romania, earning Shodan in 1990 and Nidan in 1994 with ASK in Santa Rosa. He joined DeLeon in 2008 and earned his judo Shodan in November 2014.

Sensei Brenten Sorensen

Assistant Instructor · Shodan (1st Dan)

Brenten began martial arts at 12 with Taekwondo and kickboxing, and started judo at 21 in Santa Rosa under Destinee Tartuffe at Seika Ryu. He joined DeLeon in 2017 and earned Shodan from Redwood Coast in 2018.

A non-profit club

DeLeon Judo Club is a non-profit organization. Our instructors volunteer their time. Class fees are collected by Petaluma Parks & Recreation and go toward mat time, equipment, and keeping the program affordable for families. Family discounts are available for households with more than one student.
