Privacy Policy

The short version: this is a small informational website for a volunteer-run non-profit judo club. We don't ask for your data, and we don't sell it.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

In plain English: This website has no accounts, no forms, no newsletter, no shopping cart, and no advertising trackers. If you sign up for classes, that happens on the City of Petaluma Parks & Recreation system, not here, and their privacy policy governs it.

1. Who we are

DeLeon Judo Club (“the Club,” “we,” “us”) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in Petaluma, California, operating in partnership with the City of Petaluma Parks & Recreation Department. This policy covers this website only. It does not cover the City of Petaluma, our registration provider, or any other organization we link to.

2. Information we collect

This site is a set of static pages. We do not operate accounts, logins, contact forms, mailing lists, e-commerce, or advertising on it. Specifically:

  • We do not ask you for personal information on this website. There is no form to fill out.
  • We do not set our own cookies and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers of our own.
  • Our web host may keep standard server logs — the kind every web server keeps, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and date and time. These are generated automatically by hosting infrastructure, used for security and to keep the site running, and are not used by us to identify individual visitors.

3. Information you send us voluntarily

This website does not publish instructor phone numbers or email addresses, so there is no way to send us personal information through it. If you speak to an instructor at the dojo, we will have whatever you choose to tell us — typically your name, a way to reach you, and information about the student who wants to train. We use that information only to answer you and to help you get started at the club. We do not add you to any marketing list, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

4. Third-party services on this site

Two third parties are involved when you use this website:

  • Google Maps. Our contact section embeds a Google map so you can find Herzog Hall. When that map loads, your browser contacts Google directly, and Google may set cookies or receive your IP address under its own privacy policy. If you would rather not load it, you can block third-party content in your browser — the rest of the page works fine without the map.
  • Petaluma Parks & Recreation registration (rec1.com). Our “Register” links take you off this site to the City of Petaluma's registration catalog. Any name, contact, payment, or health information you enter there is collected and held by the City and its registration provider, under their policies — not ours. We never see or store payment card information.

We do not control these services and are not responsible for their practices. Please review their privacy policies directly.

5. Children's privacy

We teach students ages 6 and up, so children are an important part of our club — but this website is written for parents and adults, and it is not directed at children as an online service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 through this website. Enrollment for a minor is completed by a parent or guardian through Petaluma Parks & Recreation. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Photographs and video

Photographs taken at the dojo, at tournaments, or at club events may appear on this website or on club materials. If you or your child appears in a photograph you would like removed, tell any instructor and we will take it down. Students and parents may also tell any instructor at any time that they prefer not to be photographed, and we will honor that.

7. How we use and share information

We use the limited information described above to respond to inquiries, run classes, and administer club membership and rank certification. We may share a student's name, rank, and membership details with the organizations that certify our members — the United States Judo Federation (USJF), our yudanshakai the Central Coast Judo Association (CENCO), and USA Judo — for the purposes of promotion records, insurance, and tournament entry. We share information with the City of Petaluma as needed to administer the program. Beyond that, we do not disclose personal information except where required by law.

8. Security and retention

We keep club records only as long as needed for club administration, rank records, and legal or insurance requirements. No website or method of storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

9. Your choices and rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by using the contact details below. California residents may have additional rights under California privacy law. Because this website itself collects no personal information for commercial purposes, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act.

10. Do Not Track

This site does not track visitors across other websites, so it does not respond differently to Do Not Track browser signals.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. How to reach us

Questions about this policy, or a request about your information, can reach us in person at the dojo.

  • In person: Herzog Hall, Sonoma-Marin Fairgrounds, 175 Fairgrounds Drive, Petaluma, CA 94952 — any Tuesday or Thursday, 5:30 pm
  • Mail: DeLeon Judo Club, PO Box 0000, Petaluma, CA 94952 Placeholder — not a real address
