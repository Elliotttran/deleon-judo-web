Judo for kids and adults, taught the traditional way since 1970.

DeLeon Judo Club has been part of the Petaluma community for over 50 years. We teach the "Gentle Way" — balance, leverage, discipline, and respect — to students ages 6 and up.

Welcome to the dojo

DeLeon Judo Club was founded in January 1970 by Jim Santiago DeLeon and has been led by Sensei Henry Kaku (Rokudan, 6th Dan) since 2007. We are a volunteer-run, non-profit club that trains at Herzog Hall on the Petaluma fairgrounds.

Our goal is simple: to give students not only the physical skills of judo, but its philosophy and traditions. We introduce judo as a lifetime pursuit — an art that can always be refined — in a dojo that is a place of stability and community.

How to join

  1. Come watch or try a Tuesday or Thursday 5:30 pm class — no experience needed.
  2. Register and pay the monthly rate through Petaluma Parks & Recreation (search "Judo"). Family discounts available.
  3. Show up in comfortable athletic clothes. We'll help you get a gi when you're ready.

What we teach

The Kodokan curriculum, in the order it was meant to be learned.

Ukemi — falling safely

The first skill every student learns, and the one they use most. Nobody is thrown before they can land.

Throws & groundwork

Nage-waza — hip throws, foot sweeps, shoulder throws — and katame-waza: pins, turnovers, escapes, and for teens and adults, chokes and arm-locks.

Randori, kata & etiquette

Free practice with a partner, the traditional forms, the Japanese terminology, and the manners that go with all of it.

The Gentle Way

Judo is the way to the most efficient use of both physical and mental energy. Through training in the attack and defense techniques of judo, the practitioner nurtures their physical and mental strength, and gradually embodies the essence of the Way of Judo. Dr. Jigoro Kano, founder of Judo

Judo uses leverage, balance, movement, and your opponent's own strength. Its two guiding principles — Maximum Efficiency and Mutual Welfare and Benefit — are as much about how you live as how you throw.
